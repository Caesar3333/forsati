# Roles and Dashboards

Forsati ships role-specific dashboards inspired by the provided admin and SaaS UI references. Each role sees only the modules allowed by its permissions and scope.

## Platform roles
### Admin Owner
Primary modules:
- KPI overview (users, orgs, revenue, providers)
- Pending approvals (opportunities, providers, programs)
- Billing and usage overview
- Audit logs and system actions
- Role and permission editor
- System settings (logo, SEO, gateways, notifications, language, extensions, sitemap, robots, GDPR, maintenance, custom CSS)

### Super Admin
Primary modules:
- Operations dashboard
- User and organization management
- Opportunity moderation
- Billing and refunds
- Content and pages
- Reports and audit logs

### Moderator
Primary modules:
- Pending content queue
- Reported users/items
- Action history
- Ticket triage (read-only unless assigned)

### Publisher / Content Manager
Primary modules:
- Blog management
- FAQ management
- Static pages and policy pages
- Frontend content blocks

### Finance / Billing Admin
Primary modules:
- Plans and subscriptions
- Invoices and refunds
- Payouts and marketplace revenue
- Payment gateways health

### Support Agent
Primary modules:
- Support ticket list/detail
- Ticket assignment/closure
- User lookup (read-only)

## Marketplace providers
### Interview Coach / Resume Reviewer / Training Provider
Primary modules:
- Verification status and documents
- Service catalog
- Orders and deliverables
- Earnings and payouts
- Reviews and ratings

## Organizations
### Organization Owner
Primary modules:
- Team and roles
- Opportunities and programs
- Applicants pipeline
- Subscription usage

### HR Manager
Primary modules:
- Job/opportunity management
- Applicants pipeline and notes
- Interview scheduling

### Recruiter
Primary modules:
- Candidate search
- Applicant tracking and notes

### Interviewer
Primary modules:
- Assigned interviews
- Scorecards and feedback

### Opportunity Manager
Primary modules:
- Volunteer/scholarship/program management
- Approval workflow

## End users
### Job Seeker / Graduate
Primary modules:
- Smart matches (jobs, volunteer, scholarship, programs)
- Applications tracking
- Resume builder and templates
- Interview prep and AI tools
- Usage limits and plan upgrades

### Volunteer / Scholarship Applicant
Primary modules:
- Opportunity discovery
- Application tracking
- Document uploads and status
