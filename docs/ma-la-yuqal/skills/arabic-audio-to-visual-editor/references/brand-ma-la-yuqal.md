# Brand: «ما لا يُقال» (Hammam Deek)

Use this when the show is «ما لا يُقال» or the user does not give another brand. If the user
connects a brand kit (Canva or similar) or uploads brand files, those win over this page.

## Tone
Deep, cinematic, contemplative. Philosophical/psychological. Slow rhythm, room to breathe.
The meaning and the feeling come first; nothing should feel like fast social-media editing.

## Colour tokens
| token | hex | use |
|---|---|---|
| bg | `#0B0D10` | base black (blue-tinted) |
| bg-2 | `#10181B` | teal-black panels/gradients |
| ivory | `#EDE6D6` | all body text |
| copper | `#B8835A` | the one emphasised word or phrase |
| grey | `#8A8F94` / `#9A9590` | secondary text, the name under the logo |

Grade: lifted blacks, teal shadows, warm amber practicals, desaturated, 3–5 % grain, soft vignette.
Colour arc follows the meaning: cold and desaturated while the problem is stated, warming at the
turning point, neutral black with a copper touch at the outro.

## Type
- Body: **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** — Light 300 for text, SemiBold 600 for the emphasised word.
  Alternative for a literary feel: Noto Naskh Arabic / Amiri.
- Logo «ما لا يُقال»: **Aref Ruqaa Ink** (رقعة), ivory with a faint copper glow, a thin copper
  rule under it, then «همام ديك» in Plex Light, grey.
- 3–5 words per line, max 2 lines, 64–80 px at 1080 width; climax line ~88 px alone.
- Never add letter-spacing to Arabic (it breaks letter joining). Never split a word into
  per-letter spans. Always `dir="rtl" lang="ar"`.

## Motion rules
- Phrases, not word-by-word pops. Calm karaoke: the whole phrase at 55 % opacity, each spoken
  word rises to 100 %.
- Text in: 500 ms blur(8px)→sharp + 8 px upward drift. Out: 400 ms. Appear ~100 ms before the word.
- No bounce, shake, flashes, neon, bright yellow/red highlights, scrolling text.
- B-roll moves very slowly (2–7 % push over a whole scene); parallax 2.5D where depth allows.
- Text sits in negative space; keep the bottom 20 % and the right edge clear of platform UI.
  Keep key cover text inside the centre 4:5 band (y 285–1635) for the Instagram grid crop.

## Sound
- Voice at −14 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP. HPF 80 Hz, gentle 2:1–3:1 compression.
- Music: drones/pads, no beat, 15 LU under the voice, ducked ~5 dB while he speaks, with
  content around 100–250 Hz so phones still hear it.
- The strongest line gets real silence after it (music and SFX to zero, room tone only).
- Transitions: soft whooshes, reverse swells, a low sub hit on the key word. Outro: one low
  piano note with a long tail.

## Identity (when Hammam appears)
Short dark hair with receding temples, trimmed beard, moustache with goatee (سكسوكة), grey hairs
in the beard, and a small dark **mole above the left corner of his upper lip** — on the
image-right side when he faces the camera. Natural skin texture, never beautified. Clothes may
change; features and natural movement may not.
